# CaliPark

CaliPark is a calisthenics training app with a map of outdoor places to train. Terms are given in English, the language of the code, with the Polish word used in the UI.

## Language

**Park** (UI: „Park”):
A public outdoor place with at least one pull-up bar or set of parallel bars for calisthenics. An outdoor gym that only has machines such as ellipticals or rowers is not a Park.
_Avoid_: Siłownia, siłownia plenerowa, gym, outdoor gym

**Account** (UI: „Konto”):
A person's identity in the Community. Training never requires an Account.
_Avoid_: Login, user profile

**Community** (UI: „Społeczność”):
Everything that people with an Account publish about Parks.
_Avoid_: Social, feed

**Nickname** (UI: „Pseudonim”):
The public name of an Account, shown next to everything it publishes. It is the only public identity of an Account.
_Avoid_: Username, display name, real name

**Submission** (UI: „Zgłoszenie”):
A proposal of a new Park, made by a person with an Account. It stays private until a Moderator accepts it.
_Avoid_: Request, suggestion, report

**Moderator** (UI: „Moderator”):
A person with an Account who accepts or rejects Submissions and handles reports about Community content.
_Avoid_: Admin
